Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Find straightforward answers about consultations, daycare options, and treatment costs at Revive Healthcare Hospital.

Call 089510 16007
How soon can I get a doctor's consultation?

Our hospital team arranges same-day or next-day consultations depending on doctor availability. For urgent appendix pain or severe bleeding, please call our 24/7 emergency helpline immediately at 089510 16007.

Are same-day discharge options available for surgical procedures?

Yes, for suitable patients undergoing minimally invasive procedures (such as laser piles treatment or select laparoscopic repairs), daycare surgery allows you to return home comfortably on the same day after post-procedure evaluation.

How do I find out about treatment costs?

We believe in clear information about treatment costs before any decision is made. During your consultation, our team will discuss available treatment options and explain expected costs openly so you can plan comfortably.

Are the treatments painful?

Our surgeons prioritize comfortable care using modern, minimally invasive techniques such as laser proctology and laparoscopic keyhole surgery, which significantly reduce pain and allow quicker recovery compared to traditional open surgery.

What should I do if I suspect appendicitis?

Sudden or worsening lower right abdominal pain is an emergency. Do not wait for a routine callback or take pain medications that might mask symptoms. Seek immediate medical evaluation at our hospital or call 089510 16007.

How can I book an appointment or ask a question?

You can fill out the enquiry form above, message us on WhatsApp at +919429690479, or call our direct hospital helpline at 089510 16007.
