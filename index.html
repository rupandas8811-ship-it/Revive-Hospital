Frequently asked questions

These answers are general information. A clinician can assess your individual symptoms.

Call the hospital
What symptoms should prompt a consultation for piles?

Bleeding, itching, discomfort or a lump around the anus can have different causes. Arrange an evaluation rather than assuming the cause, especially if bleeding persists or returns.

How is an anal fissure evaluated?

A clinician will ask about your symptoms and may perform an examination when appropriate. The assessment helps determine which next steps may be suitable for you.

When should someone consult a doctor for a fistula?

Ongoing pain, swelling or drainage near the anus should be discussed with a clinician. Seek prompt medical advice if symptoms worsen or you feel unwell.

Do all gallstones require treatment?

Not necessarily. Whether treatment is needed depends on symptoms and clinical assessment. A clinician can discuss your situation and options.

When should a hernia be evaluated?

A new or changing bulge, discomfort or pain should be assessed. Sudden severe pain or rapidly worsening symptoms need urgent medical attention.
