Clinical Indicators

When to seek a vascular assessment

Varicose veins are a progressive vascular disorder. Untreated venous hypertension can lead to irreversible skin hyperpigmentation, eczema, and painful stasis ulcers.

Bulging, Tortuous Veins

Bluish or purple cords that twist and swell beneath the surface of the thighs, knees, or calves when standing upright.

Heavy, Aching Legs & Fatigue

A deep, persistent throbbing sensation, nocturnal leg cramps, and restless legs that worsen toward evening or after standing.

Skin Darkening & Venous Eczema

Brownish discoloration (hemosiderin staining) around the ankles, itchy leathery skin, and hardening of subcutaneous fat.

Non-Healing Venous Ulcers

Chronic skin breakdown near the medial malleolus (inner ankle) that fails to heal due to elevated retrograde venous pressure.

Frequently asked questions about varicose veins

What causes varicose veins?

Veins have one-way bicuspid valves designed to pump blood upward from the legs back toward the heart against gravity. When these valves become weak or damaged, blood flows backward and pools in the veins (venous reflux), leading to stretching, bulging, and varicose veins.

Can varicose veins be cured without surgery?

Conservative therapies like medical compression stockings and leg elevation help alleviate symptoms like aching and swelling, but they cannot repair damaged venous valves. Endovenous Laser Ablation (EVLA) is the definitive, permanent gold-standard cure that seals the faulty vein safely without open surgery.

What happens to the blood flow after the vein is sealed?

The diseased vein carries only stagnant or refluxing blood. Once closed with laser energy, healthy deep and superficial veins immediately reroute the blood back toward the heart efficiently, improving overall circulation and leg lightness.

Will I be able to walk after laser treatment?

Yes! In fact, walking immediately after the procedure is encouraged to promote healthy venous circulation. Patients typically walk out of the hospital within 60 to 90 minutes and resume office work the next day.
