Understand Your Symptoms

Common Signs of Piles & When to See a Doctor

Piles (haemorrhoids) happen when blood vessels near the anus swell up. Knowing the signs early helps you get comfortable, simple relief before symptoms get worse.

01

Bright Red Bleeding

Fresh red blood drops in the toilet bowl or on tissue during or after passing stools. Usually painless in early stages, but should always be checked by a doctor.

02

Swelling or Lumps Near the Anus

A soft lump or swelling that you can feel while washing. In early stages it goes back inside by itself; in later stages it may stay outside or cause aching.

03

Itching, Discomfort & Mucus

Irritation, itching, or a feeling that bowel movements are incomplete, making sitting down or working uncomfortable throughout the day.

i Doctor's Advice: Any bleeding during stools should be examined by a qualified doctor to give you the right diagnosis and peace of mind.

Is piles treatment painful?

Our team focuses on comfortable surgical care. Modern laser treatment for piles involves minimal tissue disruption and significantly less post-procedure pain compared to traditional open surgery, allowing for a smooth and comfortable recovery.

Ask about same-day discharge options for piles?

Yes, many patients undergoing modern laser piles treatment are eligible for same-day discharge. Our doctors will assess your general health and condition during your consultation to explain the expected discharge time.

Do all stages of piles need surgery?

No. Early-stage piles can often be treated without surgery using fiber-rich diets, stool softeners, warm water baths, and medical ointments. Surgery or laser care is only suggested when symptoms persist or piles prolapse.

How can I know the exact cost of piles treatment?

We provide clear information about treatment costs during your consultation. Because costs depend on whether conservative medication, laser procedure, or surgical care is needed, our team explains all available options and costs openly before you decide.

How soon can I return to work after piles laser treatment?

Most patients who undergo laser daycare treatment can resume light routine activities and office work within 2 to 4 days, following simple dietary and hydration advice given by our team.
