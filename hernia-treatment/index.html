Signs & Symptoms

How to Recognize a Hernia

Hernias typically develop in areas where muscular walls have natural openings or previous surgical incisions.

01

Visible Bulge or Swelling

A soft lump or bulge in the groin, scrotum, navel, or around an old surgical scar that becomes more pronounced when standing or straining.

02

Aching & Dragging Sensation

A heavy, dull ache or burning discomfort in the groin or abdomen that worsens after prolonged standing, coughing, or heavy lifting.

03

Reducibility (In early stages)

The bulge often disappears or can be gently pushed back in when lying down flat, but gradually enlarges over months if left unrepaired.

i A hernia that suddenly becomes extremely painful, turns dark or red, or cannot be pushed back requires urgent emergency medical evaluation.

Repair Principles

How Hernias are Repaired at Revive

Surgery is the only definitive treatment for a hernia. The goal is to return the protruded tissue to its proper position and reinforce the weakened abdominal wall with surgical mesh.

  • ✓Laparoscopic Hernia RepairMinimally invasive keyhole repair using small incisions, providing less post-operative pain and rapid return to everyday activities.
  • ✓Mesh ReinforcementUse of biocompatible surgical mesh creates a strong, tension-free repair, substantially lowering the risk of recurrence.
  • ✓Open Hernia RepairPreferred for certain complex, large, or previously operated recurrent hernias where open access is safest.
  • ✓Post-Surgical GuidanceClear advice on lifting restrictions, abdominal support, and safe resumption of physical exercise.

Frequently Asked Questions About Hernias

Helpful medical information regarding hernia diagnosis, surgical timing, and recovery.

Can a hernia heal on its own or go away with exercise?

No. A hernia is a mechanical defect in the muscle wall. Exercise, medicines, or supportive belts (trusses) cannot close the muscle opening and will not cure a hernia. Surgery is the only definitive repair.

What is the difference between open and laparoscopic hernia repair?

Open hernia repair involves a single incision over the bulge, while laparoscopic repair utilizes tiny keyhole incisions, a camera, and specialized instruments, often resulting in less post-operative soreness and faster recovery.

When does a hernia become a medical emergency?

If the protruding tissue becomes trapped and cannot be reduced (incarceration) or loses its blood supply (strangulation), it causes sudden, severe pain, nausea, and vomiting. This is a medical emergency requiring immediate surgical intervention.

Why is surgical mesh used in hernia repair?

Surgical mesh acts like a scaffold across the weak spot, reinforcing tissue without pulling surrounding muscles tightly together (tension-free repair). This drastically reduces the likelihood of the hernia recurring.

How soon can I return to normal activity after hernia surgery?

Most patients can walk comfortably within hours of surgery and resume light desk work within 5 to 7 days. Strenuous exercise and heavy lifting should usually be avoided for 4 to 6 weeks.
